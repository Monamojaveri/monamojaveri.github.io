MONA MOJAVERI

Sculptor | Tehran, Iran

About Statement Works CV Contact

Mona Mojaveri (b. Iran) is a multidisciplinary sculptor based in Tehran. Her practice explores the intersection of fragility and violence, creating figures and objects that act as physical manifestations of internal conflict. Moving fluidly between bronze, ceramic, fiberglass, and mixed media, she selects materials that best translate the psychological and social tensions she investigates.
